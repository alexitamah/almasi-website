Who We Are

Development first.Results that speak.

Almasi Football Academy is Abuja's premier youth development programme. We identify raw talent from across Africa and provide the coaching, competition and professional infrastructure to turn potential into opportunity.

Our Mission

To identify, develop and elevate elite young footballers from across Africa, providing a genuine professional pathway to the global game.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted scout-facing development academy — where talent from every corner of the continent gets the visibility it deserves.

🌍
Two players already signed in Europe.Modoulamin Mass (ETO FC Győr, Hungary) and Edward Jassey (SK Sigma Olomouc, Czechia) — proof the pipeline from Abuja to the global stage is real and working.
